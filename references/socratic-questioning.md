# Socratic Questioning - Product Thinking Method

## Overview

Socratic questioning is the foundation of product thinking. It helps uncover hidden assumptions, clarify模糊 concepts, and explore edge cases.

## Question Types

### 1. Clarification Questions
- "What do you mean by...?"
- "How does that work?"
- "Can you give me an example?"

### 2. Probing Assumptions
- "What assumptions are we making?"
- "Why do we assume that?"
- "What could we assume instead?"

### 3. Probing Reasons and Evidence
- "What reasons support this?"
- "How do we know this is true?"
- "What evidence would change your mind?"

### 4. Exploring Implications
- "What would happen if...?"
- "What are the consequences?"
- "How does this affect other parts?"

### 5. Meta Questions
- "What was the question again?"
- "Are we asking the right question?"
- "What information do we still need?"

## Application in think vNext

During product thinking sessions, use these questions to:
1. Explore the problem space thoroughly
2. Identify conflicting requirements
3. Uncover hidden constraints
4. Validate assumptions

## Best Practices

- Ask one question at a time
- Build on previous answers
- Challenge assumptions respectfully
- Document insights for later reference
