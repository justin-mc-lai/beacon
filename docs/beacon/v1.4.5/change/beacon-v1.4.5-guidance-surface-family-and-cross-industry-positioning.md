## Why This Change Exists

Beacon already has multiple support/review surfaces, but they are still easier to read as software-adjacent exceptions than as one formal guidance family. This weakens Beacon's ability to guide non-software work without spawning new lifecycle stages.

## Delta

- define a shared decision loop above software-specific lifecycle materialization
- unify route explainability across guidance surfaces
- preserve non-truth / non-gate boundaries

## Freeze Intent

This change freezes product positioning and guidance-surface semantics. It does not add a new public lifecycle stage or replace the software delivery chain.
