# Research Closure Checklist

This is a non-canonical support reference for humans.

Use it when a feature still needs research support, but you want to know whether the research is actually decision-usable.

## Checklist

- the problem framing is explicit
- source inputs and their relevance are explicit
- architecture explanation is explicit
- business flow explanation is explicit when actors/handoffs matter
- state-machine explanation is explicit when states/transitions matter
- the key tradeoffs are visible
- the research produces route implications, not only observations
- open questions are named and bounded
- if the research changes accepted scope or promise, route to `beacon-change`

## Route Hint

- framing still weak -> `beacon-brainstorm`
- research strong enough to shape truth -> `beacon-prd` or `beacon-change`
