# Requirement truth prose norms (English)

> **角色**：速查摘要，**非**润色权威。权威全文见全局 humanizer 配置指向的 oss-sync SKILL（zh: Humanizer-zh / en: blader/humanizer）。v1.6.10: `truth-humanizer-runtime-v1610`.

Distilled from [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) and [Humanizer-zh](https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh).  
**Scope**: L0 plain-language sections and narrative prose. Tables of ACs/FSMs are exempt from rhetoric rules.

## Five rules

1. Cut fillers (Furthermore, It is important to note, In conclusion)
2. Cut puffery (pivotal, seamless, robust, evolving landscape — when empty)
3. Avoid rule-of-three stacks and fake depth -ing tails
4. Say who / does what / how we know done
5. Trust the reader

## Watchlist (narrative)

stands as a testament, underscores the importance, industry reports show (unsourced), experts believe (unsourced)

## L0 template

```markdown
## Plain language

One-line intent.

- Can: …
- Cannot: …
- Done when: …
```

## Hard boundary

- Do not drop AC IDs, illegal transitions, or state names to "sound human"
- L0 is never gate authority
